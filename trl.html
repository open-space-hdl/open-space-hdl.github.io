What the levels mean for our IP cores

The levels are those of ISO 16290, adopted by ECSS as ECSS-E-AS-11C. The ECSS handbook ECSS-E-HB-11A explains how to apply them; for components such as IP cores, its Annex B (Table B-1) lists the milestones of every level, and ESA uses this table for IP cores as well. Our IP cores are VHDL soft IP for FPGAs: properties of the device such as the package, the temperature range, total ionising dose (TID) and single-event latch-up (SEL) are covered by the qualification of the target FPGA, and the IP core has to show that it works on that device.
